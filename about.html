Independent game development

About SGgames

SGgames is an independent game studio focused on creating memorable worlds, interesting characters and games designed around player enjoyment.

Building Games From the Ground Up

SGgames began as a personal game-development project and is growing into an independent studio with multiple games, characters, worlds and creative services.

The main project is Tyrant's World, an open-world fantasy RPG featuring races, classes, monsters, bosses, abilities, multiplayer systems and a large amount of original lore.

SGgames also creates smaller projects such as Zombie Hunt. These smaller games make it possible to experiment with new ideas while continuing development on larger projects.

The Mission

SGgames aims to bring back the feeling of the golden age of gaming by creating games built around fun, discovery, challenge and memorable experiences rather than predatory monetisation or pay-to-win systems.

What SGgames Stands For

The values guiding every project.

🎮

Gameplay First

Games should be enjoyable to play. Mechanics, progression, combat and exploration should create a strong experience instead of existing only to sell additional content.

🌍

Original Worlds

Every project should have its own identity, characters, lore and atmosphere instead of feeling like a copy of something that already exists.

🛡️

No Pay to Win

Player success should come from skill, strategy, exploration and progression rather than spending more money than other players.

🧪

Experimentation

Smaller games and unusual mechanics make it possible to test ideas, learn new systems and discover what creates enjoyable gameplay.

📖

Strong Lore

Characters, monsters, locations and abilities should feel connected to the world and have reasons for existing beyond their gameplay function.

🚀

Long-Term Growth

SGgames is being developed gradually, with every released game, new system and completed project helping the studio move toward larger goals.

The Development Journey

Important stages in the growth of SGgames.

Learning Game Development

2

Tyrant's World Begins

The first major SGgames project began as an ambitious fantasy RPG containing an open world, character progression, bosses, magic and multiplayer features.

3

Public Store Presence

Tyrant's World received a public Steam store page, allowing players to discover the project and add it to their wishlists during development.

4

Smaller Games and Services

Projects such as Zombie Hunt and custom character services allow SGgames to release smaller work, test new ideas and continue building experience.

5

The Future

The long-term goal is to grow SGgames into a larger studio capable of creating more ambitious games and eventually expanding into additional creative projects.

About the Founder

SGgames was founded by an independent developer from Norway with experience in game development, programming, 3D modelling, website creation, video production and online content.

The studio is currently being built through practical projects, continuous learning and the goal of turning original ideas into finished games that people can play.

Development includes work with engines and tools such as Unreal Engine, Blender, programming software and digital design tools.
