The adventure begins

A fantasy world built around freedom

Tyrant's World is designed to let players create a character, explore a dangerous world and grow through combat, discovery, quests, abilities and the choices they make along the way.

Choose a race and class, improve your stats, unlock new abilities and find equipment that changes how you approach each fight. Travel alone or join other players for difficult encounters and shared adventures.

The world contains peaceful settlements, hostile wilderness, hidden dungeons and enemies powerful enough to become legends. Some threats can be defeated through strength. Others demand preparation, teamwork and knowledge of how their powers work.

Regions of the world

Every place has a story

From living forests to lands ruled by the dead, each region is meant to feel distinct in atmosphere, danger and lore.

Wilderness region

Forest of the Druids

A beautiful but dangerous forest carrying the scars of ancient fire, hidden creatures and the druids who once called it home.

Realm of the dead

The Necropolis

A dark kingdom connected to Hades, restless spirits and powers that can destroy a person without ever drawing a blade.

Great city

Utopia

A grand city where travellers, merchants and powerful figures meet beneath the promise of safety and progress.

Legends and nightmares

Enemies remembered by name

Tyrant's World is filled with villains and monsters whose powers, histories and personalities make them more than simple obstacles.

Force of nature

Ifrit

A sealed being whose flames are so powerful that only he can extinguish them.

Lord of corruption

Hades

A manipulator who turns kindness, faith and courage into weapons against the people who possess them.

Undead terror

A feared creature whose presence can break the courage of even experienced adventurers.

Vampire lord

A powerful vampire whose name, bloodline and influence reach far beyond a single battle.

Demon king
