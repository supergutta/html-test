Dark God

Ifrit

The Unquenchable Flame

An ancient force of nature whose flames are powerful enough to drive entire civilizations from their homes and burn long after every ordinary fire should have died.

Ifrit, the ancient half-human and half-demon fire being sealed away for approximately two thousand years.

Character Overview

Ifrit is not simply a powerful enemy. He is a force of nature whose presence can reshape entire regions. His body is formed from ash, heat and living flame, and the fire he creates cannot be extinguished by normal means.

Even devils fear the heat of Ifrit's flames. Once they begin burning, only Ifrit himself can truly command them to stop.

History

Long ago, Ifrit's flames devastated the homeland of the druids and forced them to flee. His power was too great for ordinary warriors, mages or armies to overcome.

Merlin and Vise attempted to stop him, but even their combined power was not enough to destroy him. In the end, the Four Holy Mages were required to seal Ifrit away rather than kill him.

Nature and Personality

Ifrit behaves less like a traditional villain and more like a natural disaster given thought and purpose. He does not need armies, wealth or political influence. His existence alone is enough to threaten the world around him.

He carries the patience of an immortal being and the destructive rage of a wildfire. He understands that most mortals cannot truly stop him, only delay him.

Powers and Abilities

  • Unquenchable Flame

    Ifrit creates supernatural fire that cannot be extinguished by water, ordinary magic or natural weather. Only Ifrit can fully put it out.

  • Ash-Fire Body

    His body is formed from ash and living flame, making conventional physical attacks unreliable against him.

  • Infernal Heat

    The temperature surrounding Ifrit can become so extreme that approaching him may be dangerous before he even attacks.

  • Firestorm

    Ifrit can spread fire across a vast battlefield, turning safe areas into burning hazards and controlling where his enemies are able to move.

  • Immortal Djinn

    His connection to the djinn makes him extremely difficult, and possibly impossible, to kill through ordinary means.

A Threat That Cannot Be Fought Normally

Ifrit is designed as a dark-god villain. Players cannot defeat him through ordinary damage and combat alone. Surviving him requires preparation, ancient knowledge, powerful magic and a method of containing or redirecting his destructive force.

Combat Style

Ifrit controls large areas with flames, explosions, waves of heat and burning terrain. His encounter is focused on survival, positioning and understanding when direct confrontation is impossible.

Players may need to use environmental protection, concentrated ice magic or ancient sealing techniques rather than attempting to overpower him.
