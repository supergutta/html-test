Undead Boss

Shinigami

The Bound Undead

A terrifying undead being who hunts the strongest member of every group, spreads fear across the battlefield and longs for the death that may finally release him.

Shinigami, a powerful undead boss whose terrifying appearance hides a being trapped in endless suffering.

Character Overview

Shinigami is a powerful undead creature bound to an existence he can no longer escape. He appears as a merciless hunter, but beneath the horror is a being desperate for permanent death.

He does not attack randomly. Shinigami identifies the strongest person nearby and focuses his attention on them, treating their power as both a challenge and a possible path toward freedom.

Personality

Shinigami appears violent, relentless and cruel. He rarely shows hesitation and treats combat as if every battle may finally bring an end to his existence.

His aggression comes from suffering rather than simple hatred. He has endured undeath for so long that victory and defeat have become equally meaningless unless one of them grants him freedom.

Powers and Abilities

  • Hunter of the Strong

    Shinigami identifies the highest-level player and treats them as his main target until they die or the encounter ends.

  • Scream of Terror

    He releases a supernatural scream that inflicts fear, disrupts movement and can cause players to lose control temporarily.

  • Deathly Presence

    Players standing near Shinigami feel their courage weaken, making it harder to fight effectively while remaining close to him.

  • Soul Pursuit

    Shinigami can rapidly close the distance between himself and his chosen target, preventing the strongest player from escaping easily.

  • Undead Endurance

    His undead body allows him to continue fighting after injuries that would kill most creatures.

  • Final Desperation

    When his health becomes low, Shinigami becomes faster and more aggressive as he senses that permanent death may finally be close.

The Prison of Undeath

Shinigami's greatest enemy is not the player. It is the curse that prevents him from truly dying. Every battle he fights is another attempt to find someone strong enough to end his suffering.

This makes his defeat different from an ordinary boss victory. When players finally destroy him, Shinigami does not curse them or promise revenge. Instead, he realizes that his long imprisonment is finally over.

Combat Style

Shinigami's encounter separates the strongest player from the rest of the group. That player must survive his direct attacks while the others manage fear effects, interrupt screams and create opportunities to damage him.

Because he changes targets based on player level rather than distance, the party cannot simply hide its strongest member behind a tank. The group must protect whoever Shinigami has chosen.

— Shinigami's final words
