Half-Blood Vampire

Nyx Bloodfallen

The Independent Heir

A half-human and half-vampire woman who walks in daylight, ages far more slowly than ordinary people and refuses to become a servant of her father's bloodline.

Nyx Bloodfallen, a rare half-vampire who possesses many of the strengths of her kind without all of their weaknesses.

Character Overview

Nyx Bloodfallen is the daughter of a human and a powerful vampire. Her mixed blood allows her to survive sunlight and move among humans more easily than a full-blooded vampire.

However, her human side does not remove her hunger. When Nyx feeds, she can continue draining a victim until nothing remains unless she deliberately stops herself.

Personality

Nyx is independent, confident and unwilling to accept control simply because of her bloodline. She does not automatically obey her father or consider vampires superior to humans.

She can be calm and intelligent, but her hunger creates a dangerous side that she must constantly manage. Nyx understands that one moment of lost control can turn a simple feeding into a death.

Powers and Abilities

  • Blood Drain

    Nyx can drain blood from a living target to restore her strength and satisfy her hunger. She may continue feeding until the victim dies.

  • Sunlight Tolerance

    Unlike most full vampires, Nyx can move through daylight without burning or losing all of her strength.

  • Superhuman Strength

    Her vampire blood grants her physical power beyond that of an ordinary human.

  • Accelerated Healing

    Nyx can recover from wounds more quickly than humans, especially after consuming fresh blood.

  • Heightened Senses

    Her hearing, sight and sense of smell allow her to detect nearby movement, danger and blood.

  • Slow Ageing

    Nyx ages approximately twenty times more slowly than an ordinary human, allowing her to live for centuries while remaining physically young.

The Danger of Feeding

Nyx does not merely take a small amount of blood by instinct. Once she begins feeding, her vampire nature pushes her to continue until the victim has been fully drained. Stopping requires self-control rather than a natural limit.

A Daughter Without Loyalty

Nyx is connected to one of the world's most dangerous vampire bloodlines, but she does not consider herself her father's servant. Her actions are based on her own goals, relationships and judgement.

This makes her unpredictable. She may oppose her father, help the player or act entirely for herself depending on the situation. Her blood does not decide her loyalty.

Combat Style

Nyx fights with speed, close-range strength and attacks that allow her to recover health by draining enemies. She can remain active during daylight, making her more flexible than most vampire enemies.

Her danger increases once she reaches a wounded target. A successful feeding can restore her strength and turn a nearly defeated Nyx back into a serious threat.

Story Potential

Nyx can become an ally, rival or enemy depending on the player's choices. Her independence allows her to appear in vampire storylines without automatically supporting the vampire lord.

Players may have to decide whether they trust her, attempt to control her hunger or judge her based on the actions of her family.

“My blood tells me what I am. It does not decide who I serve.”
— Nyx Bloodfallen
