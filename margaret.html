Major Villain

Margaret

The Puppet Master

A mysterious and dangerous woman who transforms every battle into a performance and forces everyone around her to play a part in her twisted show.

Margaret the Puppet Master, one of the major villains of Tyrant's World.

Character Overview

Margaret is a mysterious and dangerous woman who controls puppets and bends others to her will. She turns the battlefield into a stage where everyone else is forced to perform.

To Margaret, combat is not simply about defeating an opponent. It is a carefully prepared performance in which every movement, scream and death is another part of the show.

Basic Information

Full Name Margaret
Title The Puppet Master
Role Major Villain
Alignment Evil
Main Power Puppet Manipulation
Theme Dark Fantasy and Horror

Personality

Margaret is calm, theatrical and controlling. She rarely loses her composure because she prefers to make others feel powerless before the battle has even properly begun.

She treats people as tools, actors and unfinished puppets. Those who resist her control become challenges she is determined to break.

Powers and Abilities

  • Puppet Manipulation

    Margaret controls enchanted puppets using supernatural strings, allowing her to attack from several directions at once.

  • Living Marionette

    She can attempt to control the movements of living targets, turning allies against each other or forcing victims into danger.

  • Cursed Strings

    Her strings can bind, pull and suspend enemies, limiting their movement and leaving them open to attacks from her puppets.

  • The Final Performance

    Margaret fills the battlefield with puppets and strings, transforming the entire area into her personal stage.

Combat Style

Margaret avoids fighting like an ordinary warrior. She controls the battlefield from a distance, traps players with strings and sends puppets to attack from several positions.

Her encounter focuses on movement, awareness and deciding which threats must be destroyed first before the number of puppets becomes overwhelming.

“Every great performance requires actors. Do not worry. I have already chosen your role.”
— Margaret, the Puppet Master
