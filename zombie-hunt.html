Game overview

Survival without room for mistakes

Zombie Hunt is a fast, compact survival game built around limited resources, escalating pressure and a deadly infection system.

You have 30 seconds to prepare before the first wave begins. Once the undead arrive, every bullet, cure and second matters.

There is no traditional health bar. A zombie bite infects you, starting a ten-second countdown. Find and use a cure in time or become one of the zombies yourself.

Different zombie types force you to change how you defend yourself. Some rush directly at you, while others think, dig, build or protect themselves with armor.

Core features

Every wave changes the pressure

Simple rules create increasingly tense decisions as supplies disappear and stronger enemies enter the fight.

Ⅰ

Twenty survival waves

Hold out through a complete night of escalating attacks and see whether you can remain human until the final wave.

Ⅱ

Limited supplies

Weapons, ammunition and cures are valuable. Waste them early and the later waves become much harder to survive.

Ⅲ

No health bar

Survival is not about absorbing damage. Avoid getting bitten, because infection—not lost health—is the true danger.

Ⅳ

Ten-second infection

A bite starts a countdown. Reach a cure before it ends or your run is over and your character joins the undead.

Ⅴ

Different zombie behaviors

Zombies do more than move toward you. Specialized enemies can think, dig, build defenses or absorb more damage.

Ⅵ

Fast arcade survival

Easy to understand and quick to begin, while the later waves reward planning, movement and careful resource use.

Infection system

A bite starts the real countdown

Zombie Hunt replaces a normal health system with a direct, visible consequence for being caught by the undead.

10 Seconds remaining

Find a cure before time runs out

Getting bitten does not immediately end the run, but it begins a short emergency. Your priorities change instantly: escape the zombies, reach a cure and use it before the timer ends.

Zombie types

The horde does not fight one way

Five enemy types create different threats and prevent one simple strategy from carrying you through every wave.

01

Normal Zombie

The basic member of the horde. Individually manageable, but dangerous when several surround you.

02

Smart Zombie

Watches the situation and behaves less predictably than normal undead, forcing you to stay alert.

03

Digging Zombie

Uses the ground to approach or bypass parts of your defense, making safe positions less reliable.

04

Armored Zombie

Protected by armor and harder to bring down, consuming resources you may need for the waves ahead.

05

Builder Zombie

Changes the battlefield by creating structures that help the horde and interfere with your survival plan.

Survival loop

Prepare, fight, adapt, repeat

Each part of the run feeds into the next. Decisions made in an early wave can decide whether you survive the final one.

Prepare

Use the 30-second opening window to understand the area and get ready before the first zombies arrive.

Defend

Keep moving, control the horde and avoid being trapped by groups or specialized zombie types.

Conserve

Do not waste ammunition or cures. Limited supplies make every choice more important as the night continues.

Adapt

Change your plan when new enemies appear, defenses fail or an infection suddenly becomes your highest priority.

Failure has a consequence

The player can become the enemy

When the infection countdown reaches zero, the player becomes a zombie. The undead character remains in the world instead of simply disappearing, turning the failed survivor into part of the horde.
