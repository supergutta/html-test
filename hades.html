Psychological Villain

Hades

The Forgotten Corruption

A manipulator who destroys noble people from within, forces them to commit unforgivable acts, and leaves them alive to face the consequences.

Hades, a manipulator whose victims may forget his face but never escape the damage he leaves behind.

Character Overview

Hades is not feared because he destroys cities with overwhelming strength. He is feared because he can take something kind, heroic or sacred and twist it into the opposite of what it once represented.

He enters people's lives, discovers what they value, and uses their own minds and bodies to destroy it. His greatest victories are not measured in deaths, but in the people who survive him completely broken.

Personality

Hades is calm, cold and strangely polite. He does not need to shout or lose control because he usually enters a situation believing that everyone around him is already beneath him.

He sometimes shows mercy, but his mercy is often worse than death. He may spare someone simply because living with what happened will cause them more pain.

Powers and Abilities

  • Possession

    Hades can take control of a mortal's body and force them to move, speak or attack against their will.

  • Corrupted Will

    He can weaken a person's judgement and twist noble intentions into violent or destructive actions.

  • Memory Fade

    Most mortals forget Hades approximately two minutes after he leaves their presence.

  • Chosen Memory

    Hades can decide that a person is worthy of remembering him, allowing the memory of their encounter to remain.

  • True Form

    Hades normally conceals his real appearance. Anyone who sees his true face may be marked for death unless he personally chooses to spare them.

The Curse of Being Forgotten

Hades' memory effect allows him to move through the mortal world without leaving ordinary witnesses. Victims may remember the horror, the missing people and the consequences, but lose the ability to clearly remember who caused it.

Sister Anny

After Hades lost consciousness near a church, Sister Anny found him and helped him recover. When he awoke, he believed that the people inside may have seen his true form.

Because Sister Anny had treated him kindly, Hades chose not to kill her. Instead, he possessed her and forced her to kill every man, woman and child inside her own church.

When everyone was dead, Hades returned control of her body and told her that she should be grateful to still be alive. He then left her alone with the bodies and the knowledge of what her own hands had done.

Combat Style

Hades fights by disrupting trust and control. He can possess allies, confuse players, create false targets and manipulate characters into attacking one another.

His encounter should feel less like a normal boss fight and more like surviving a psychological trap where the player's own party can become the greatest threat.

“If you were not the one helping me when I woke up, you might have been one of them. Be happy you are still alive, Sister.”
— Hades
