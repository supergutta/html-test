Arachne

The Web Queen

A monstrous spider queen who covers the battlefield in webs, poisons her victims and overwhelms intruders with cocoons, spiders and scorpions.

Arachne, the Web Queen, a dangerous boss capable of controlling movement and poisoning entire groups.

Character Overview

Arachne is a powerful spider creature who controls her territory through webs, poison and summoned monsters. Entering her domain means stepping into a battlefield she has already prepared.

She does not need to chase every victim herself. Instead, she restricts movement, surrounds players with minions and waits for her poison to weaken them.

Behaviour

Arachne is patient and territorial. She prefers to trap enemies rather than immediately killing them, allowing poison and summoned creatures to slowly remove their ability to fight back.

She treats intruders as prey and potential food for her offspring. The longer a battle lasts, the more dangerous her web-covered arena becomes.

Powers and Abilities

  • Sticky Stop

    Arachne places a massive circular web with a radius of approximately five metres. Players standing inside it have their movement speed reduced by 50%.

  • Poison Sting

    Arachne bites a target, dealing 50 immediate damage and applying poison that deals 2 damage per second for 20 seconds.

  • Stacking Poison

    Her poison can stack up to five times. Each additional stack increases the poison damage, making repeated exposure extremely dangerous.

  • Poison Spit

    Arachne can launch poison at distant targets through spit or along her webs. This applies the poison effect without the initial bite damage.

  • Cocoon Creation

    She creates cocoons using her spinnerets and places them around the battlefield to summon or contain additional threats.

  • Cocoon Launch

    Arachne can form a cocoon, take it in her hand and throw it across the battlefield toward a chosen location or target.

  • Scorpion Summoning

    She releases dangerous scorpions into the arena, forcing players to divide their attention between Arachne and her summoned creatures.

Poison Stack Warning

A single poison effect may be manageable, but allowing the effect to reach five stacks can rapidly drain a player's health. Players must avoid repeated bites, poison projectiles and contaminated webs while deciding when to use healing or poison removal.

Combat Style

Arachne's boss battle is built around controlling space. Her large webs slow players while cocoons, spiders and scorpions reduce the number of safe areas.

Players must keep moving, manage poison stacks and destroy summoned threats before the battlefield becomes impossible to navigate.

Battle Strategy

Ranged players can attack from outside her webs, but must remain alert for poison spit and launched cocoons. Close-range fighters risk being bitten and gaining multiple poison stacks.

The group must decide whether to focus entirely on Arachne or temporarily destroy her minions and cocoons to regain control of the arena.

“Struggle as much as you like. Every movement only pulls the web tighter.”
